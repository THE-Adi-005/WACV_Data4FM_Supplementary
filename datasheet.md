# Datasheet for Dataset: Say What You See (Open3DFaceVid Part-1 Grounding Audit)
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Canonical Part-1 Manifest SHA-256:** `c493484f4457ecbb2c6a1523ea811af6d887ca593d8bf6b9c99eada28b3d5cb9`
- **Upstream Source:** Open3DFaceVid (`TDMM-LM`, Song et al., arXiv:2603.16936), Part-1 synthetic face-video release (`56,187` on-disk MP4 clips; `64,228` `data_part1.json` entries)
- **Release Scope & Redistribution Policy:** This benchmark releases derivative annotations, SHA-256 checksums, `val_gold`/`test_gold`/`test_raw` manifests, `Verifiability-Cleaned (C3)` prompt rewrites, matched content-control splits (`C_Type_NoReg`, `C_Type_Reg`, `C_StratRand`, `C_Mask`, `RR`), calibrated verifiability scores, and `AGS`/`CHAIR-Video` evaluation code. Raw `.mp4` videos remain hosted by the upstream Open3DFaceVid authors under their original license/terms.
- **License (Derivative Annotations & Code):** CC BY 4.0 (annotations/metadata) / MIT License (evaluation and cleaning scripts); upstream video assets governed by Open3DFaceVid terms.
- **Evaluated Split Sizes:** `val_gold`: 192 videos (634 claims); `test_gold`: 192 videos (632 claims); `train_R`: 55,803; `train_C1`: 52,381; `train_C2`/`train_C3`/`train_C4` and controls (`C_Type_NoReg`, `C_Type_Reg`, `C_StratRand`, `C_Mask`, `RR`): 48,790.
- **Stage 3 Quality & Full-Corpus YuNet Anomaly Audit:** `qc.json` screens all `56,187` clips for brightness/motion/blur (`49,181` heuristic `keep`, `3,578` `soft_flag_blur`, `3,428` `hard_exclude`). Parallel full-corpus neural face & cel-shading screening (`detect_dataset_anomalies.py` with `cv2.FaceDetectorYN` -> `dataset_anomaly_audit.json`, synced into `qc.json`) audits all `56,187` clips (`53,490` clean single-speaker `95.2%`, `2,662` excluded `4.7%`: `1,235` multi-face, `1,097` no-face, `330` cartoon/CGI, plus `35` sparse-face soft flags; surfacing `2,344` heuristic-`keep` clips with neural anomalies while preserving `48,790` condition sizes).
- **Calibrated Claim & Cleaning Counts:** `187,455` extracted claims scored (`68,502` strictly `Supported`-groundable vs. `118,953` ungrounded at $p \ge 0.50$; strict predictor ROC-AUC `0.9122`, Brier `0.1013`, reproducible under `scikit-learn==1.8.0`); `cleaned_captions_c3.json` retains `64,447` grounded claims and drops `111,681` ungrounded claims across `52,742` usable clips.
- **Evaluation Benchmark Suite:** `AGS` / `CHAIR-Video` via validated zero-bias `extract_nl()` parser (`nl_extractor_validation.json`: `1.000` adversarial F1; `93.0%` agreement across 384 human blind descriptions) + `734` grounded 3-way `POPE-Abstain` probes (`pope_abstain_benchmark.json`: `296` Yes, `296` No, `142` Unobservable).
- **Human Verification Protocol:** 3-Pass Template-Guided Blind-First audit (401 stratified clips -> 17 anomalous quarantined, 384 clean single-speaker clips / 1,266 claims).
- **Standard Taxonomies:** ISCO-08 (Professions), Plutchik / GoEmotions (Emotions), Leary Circumplex (Style).
- **Synthetic Data & Ethical Notice:** Audited clips are T2V-generated synthetic faces paired with ChatGPT-4.1-polished prompts (`[PAPER, Sec. 3]`), containing no audio stream in Part-1 containers (`[OBSERVED]`). No identity recognition, surveillance, or demographic inference classifiers are distributed.
